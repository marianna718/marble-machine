The Marble Machine

A tiny training game about chance. Pull marbles, watch the average find you.

What this trains. The chance of each marble is just counting: 6/10, 3/10, 1/10. The expected value of a pull is each prize times its chance, added up:

(0.6 × 1) + (0.3 × 5) + (0.1 × 20) = 4.1 points

A pull costs 5 but is worth 4.1 — so the machine keeps 0.9 per pull on average, a give-back of 82%. No single pull is ever worth 4.1 (you always get exactly 1, 5, or 20); the number only becomes visible when the pulls pile up. That's the law of large numbers — and it's why one player can beat a machine, but a crowd never does.

The bag has no memory, rare doesn't mean never, and 1-in-10 doesn't mean every 10th pull. Everything else is costume.
